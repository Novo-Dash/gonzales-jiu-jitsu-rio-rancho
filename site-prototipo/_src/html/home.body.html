IICompetition · Head instructor

Two world titles.Twenty years of service.

Abie Gonzales is a black belt and two-time IBJJF No-Gi World Champion, with a background in wrestling and MMA. Before the mat came twenty years in the U.S. Army, three tours in Iraq and a Bronze Star.

He opened Gonzales Jiu-Jitsu in 2024 with his wife Laura, a former soldier and Army Combatives instructor. Their three sons train here. Every coach on this mat is IBJJF certified.
